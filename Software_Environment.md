## Scope of the checks

Both F2/F3 and F4/F5 completed when their full script paths were invoked with `Rscript` from outside the project directory. The scripts discovered `data/Table S2.xlsx` relative to their own location. The workbooks remained unchanged.

F4/F5 passed all 17 internal checks. Its 31 PDF outputs had the same extracted text, annotation order and page dimensions as the author's Windows result set. After accounting for input-path metadata and the extra title row in Table S2, the maximum absolute difference among 47,599 statistical numeric cells was approximately 1.30 × 10⁻⁶. The displayed values, fitted model forms and record counts agreed. Graphic files were not byte-identical, and rendered pixels differed across the two software environments.

F2/F3 completed with all 19 PDF and 19 PNG outputs. Its 311 invasive-versus-native source records are unchanged by the Table S2 revision. The panel treatments and retained row counts matched the documented definitions.

F1 was not rerun during this verification round. Its script and input were retained byte-for-byte from the supplied F1 package. This record therefore makes no new claim of an F1 execution under either environment above.

The scripts and dependencies are not locked to a single runtime. Package updates, numerical libraries, fonts and graphics devices can affect numerical precision or rendering. For publication, retain the `session_info` or `run_info` records generated by the actual final runs. A successful check in the listed environments is not a promise of byte-identical output on every platform.
